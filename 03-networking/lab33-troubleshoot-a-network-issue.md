# Lab 33 – Troubleshoot a Network Issue

## Scenario

As an IT helpdesk technician for a small company, I was assigned a network support ticket in Issue Trax. The issue involved a laptop in Office 2 that could not connect to the company's wireless network. I diagnosed the problem, restored wireless connectivity, documented the fix, and closed the ticket.

---

## Objectives

- Review a help desk ticket in Issue Trax.
- Test wireless connectivity from a known working computer.
- Troubleshoot wireless connectivity on the Office 2 laptop.
- Identify the cause of the wireless connection problem.
- Restore wireless network connectivity.
- Document the resolution in the help desk ticket.
- Close the support ticket.

## Procedure

1. Opened **Issue Trax** from the Start menu.
2. Maximized the Issue Trax window.
3. Opened and reviewed **Ticket #25**.
4. Tested wireless connectivity on the **ITAdmin** computer.
5. Opened the available wireless networks and selected **CorpNet**.
6. Connected the ITAdmin computer to the **CorpNet** wireless network.
7. Determined that the wireless access point was functioning because the ITAdmin computer could connect successfully.
8. Navigated to **Office 2** and selected the **Office2-Lap** laptop.
9. Checked the laptop's available network connections.
10. Determined that no wireless networks were displayed.
11. Inspected the physical wireless switch on the laptop.
12. Found that the wireless network interface switch was in the **OFF** position.
13. Switched the wireless network interface to the **ON** position.
14. Opened the laptop's available wireless networks.
15. Confirmed that the **CorpNet** wireless network was now visible.
16. Selected **CorpNet** and clicked **Connect**.
17. Verified that the laptop successfully connected to the wireless network.
18. Returned to the IT Administration Office and opened **Issue Trax**.
19. Added a professional comment to Ticket #25 describing the problem and the fix.
20. Changed the ticket status to **Closed**.

---

## Outcome

Successfully diagnosed and resolved the wireless connectivity issue on the Office2-Lap computer. The problem was caused by the laptop's wireless network interface being turned off. After enabling the wireless switch, the laptop detected and connected to the **CorpNet** wireless network. The resolution was documented in Issue Trax and Ticket #25 was closed.
