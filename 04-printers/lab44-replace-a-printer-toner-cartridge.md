# Lab 44 – Replace a Printer Toner Cartridge

## Scenario

A user reports that their printer is no longer printing yellow. After troubleshooting the issue, you determine that the yellow toner cartridge needs to be replaced. The task is to remove the existing cartridge, install a new one, and verify that the printer is ready to use.

## Objectives

- Access and remove the existing toner cartridge.
- Safely handle a new toner cartridge.
- Distribute toner inside the cartridge before installation.
- Verify printer status after replacing the cartridge.

## Technologies

- Laser printer
- Yellow toner cartridge
- Toner tray
- Printer self-diagnostic process

## Skills Demonstrated

- Replacing a toner cartridge
- Handling printer consumables safely
- Preventing print quality problems
- Performing post-maintenance printer checks

## Procedure

1. Open the printer's front cabinet door.
2. Grasp the blue handle and pull the toner tray straight out.
3. Remove the existing yellow toner cartridge.
4. Take the new yellow toner cartridge out of its box.
5. Avoid touching the roller unit on the new cartridge because oil or debris from your hands can degrade print quality.
6. Hold the cartridge horizontally and tip it back and forth to distribute the toner.
7. Install the new yellow toner cartridge in the correct position.
8. Slide the toner tray back into the printer.
9. Close the front cabinet door.
10. Check the printer status after the self-diagnosis to confirm that the printer is ready to use.

## Outcome

Successfully reviewed the procedure for replacing a yellow toner cartridge, learned how to handle the cartridge without contaminating its roller, and identified the final printer status check needed after replacement.

## Key Takeaways

- Pull the toner tray out using the blue handle after opening the front cabinet door.
- Avoid touching the toner cartridge's roller because oil and debris can affect print quality.
- Hold the cartridge horizontally and tip it back and forth to distribute toner.
- After installation, close the cabinet door and check the printer status after its self-diagnosis.
