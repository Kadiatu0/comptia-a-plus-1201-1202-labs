# Lab 45 – Clear a Paper Jam in a Printer

## Scenario

A print job sent to a local printer stopped suddenly without producing a printed page. The printer display shows a "Paper Tray 2 Jam" error. The task is to locate and remove the jammed paper, reassemble the printer, and verify that the issue is resolved.

## Objectives

- Troubleshoot a Paper Tray 2 Jam error.
- Remove jammed paper without tearing it.
- Inspect the rear duplexer if no paper is visible in the tray area.
- Reassemble the printer correctly.
- Test the printer after clearing the jam.

## Technologies

- Laser printer
- Paper Tray 2
- Rear duplexer
- Printer paper feed system

## Skills Demonstrated

- Troubleshooting printer paper jams
- Safely removing jammed paper
- Inspecting printer paper paths
- Reassembling printer components
- Verifying printer operation after troubleshooting

## Procedure

1. When the printer displays the "Paper Tray 2 Jam" error, pull Tray 2 completely out of the printer.
2. Inspect the tray area for jammed paper.
3. If paper is present, grasp it with both hands and pull carefully to avoid tearing it.
4. If no jammed paper is visible in the tray area, inspect the rear duplexer.
5. Remove any jammed paper found in the rear duplexer.
6. Close the rear duplexer.
7. Slide Tray 2 back into the printer until it is fully seated.
8. Test the printer to verify that the paper jam error has been resolved.

## Outcome

Reviewed how to troubleshoot a Paper Tray 2 Jam error by removing the tray, carefully removing jammed paper, checking the rear duplexer when necessary, and securing the printer components before testing.

## Key Takeaways

- Pull Tray 2 completely out of the printer as the first troubleshooting step.
- Use both hands to remove jammed paper carefully and prevent tearing.
- If no paper is visible in the tray area, check the rear duplexer.
- Close the rear duplexer and fully reseat Tray 2 before testing.
- Test the printer after clearing the jam to confirm normal operation.
