# Lab 16 – Troubleshoot System Startup

## Scenario

As a computer repair technician, I was tasked with troubleshooting a computer that failed to boot after a motherboard replacement. The issue was caused by incorrect connections made during the hardware upgrade.

---

## Objectives

- Diagnose startup problems after motherboard installation.
- Restore power connections.
- Connect the CPU fan to prevent overheating.
- Verify successful system boot.

---

## Technologies

- Motherboard
- CPU Fan Connector
- Power Supply
- Heat Sink and Cooling System

---

## Skills Demonstrated

- Hardware Troubleshooting
- Motherboard Installation Verification
- Power Connection Testing
- Cooling System Configuration

---

## Procedure

1. Attempted to start the computer and confirmed the startup failure.
2. Checked power connections and found the AC power cable was not connected.
3. Connected the AC power cable to the wall outlet.
4. Restarted the computer and identified an overheating shutdown issue.
5. Connected the CPU fan cable to the motherboard.
6. Powered on the computer and verified that the system remained running and successfully booted.

---

## Outcome

Successfully diagnosed and corrected startup issues caused by improper power and CPU fan connections after a motherboard replacement.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Learned the importance of verifying connections after hardware upgrades.
- Practiced troubleshooting motherboard installation issues.
- Improved understanding of CPU cooling and power requirements.
