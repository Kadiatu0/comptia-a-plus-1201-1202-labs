# Lab 21 – Troubleshoot a Malfunctioning Computer

## Scenario

As a computer repair technician, I was assigned to diagnose and repair a computer that would not boot. The issue involved both a failed power supply and faulty memory.

---

## Objectives

- Diagnose startup failures.
- Replace a faulty power supply.
- Identify and replace defective RAM.
- Restore normal system operation.

---

## Technologies

- ATX Power Supply
- DDR Memory Modules
- SATA Power Connectors
- PCIe Power Connector

---

## Skills Demonstrated

- Hardware Troubleshooting
- Power Supply Replacement
- Memory Diagnostics
- System Repair

---

## Procedure

1. Tested the computer and confirmed it would not power on.
2. Replaced the faulty power supply and reconnected:
   - Motherboard power
   - CPU power
   - PCIe power
   - SATA power connectors
3. Connected the AC power cable and turned on the new power supply.
4. Powered on the computer and identified a memory-related beep code.
5. Tested each memory module to locate the faulty RAM.
6. Replaced the defective memory module with a compatible replacement.
7. Powered on the computer and verified it successfully booted into Windows.

---

## Outcome

Successfully diagnosed and repaired the computer by replacing the failed power supply and defective memory module, restoring normal system operation.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Learned how to troubleshoot multiple hardware failures.
- Practiced replacing power supplies and RAM.
- Improved systematic hardware diagnostic skills.
