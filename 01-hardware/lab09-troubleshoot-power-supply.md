# Lab 9 – Troubleshoot Power Supply Problems

## Scenario

As an IT helpdesk technician, I was assigned to troubleshoot a computer that failed to boot. The goal was to test the power supply, identify the issue, and replace the faulty unit.

---

## Objectives

- Test a suspected faulty power supply.
- Replace the failed PSU.
- Restore system operation.

---

## Technologies

- ATX Power Supply
- Power Supply Tester
- SATA Power Connectors
- Motherboard Power Connectors

---

## Skills Demonstrated

- Power Supply Troubleshooting
- Hardware Diagnostics
- PSU Replacement
- Cable Installation

---

## Procedure

1. Attempted to power on the computer and confirmed it failed to boot.
2. Connected the power supply tester to the PSU connectors.
3. Tested the:
   - 20+4 pin motherboard connector
   - 4-pin CPU power connector
   - SATA power connector
4. Determined the power supply was faulty based on tester results.
5. Removed the defective power supply.
6. Installed a replacement ATX power supply with PCIe support.
7. Reconnected:
   - Motherboard power cable
   - CPU power cable
   - SATA power connectors
8. Powered on the system and verified successful boot.

---

## Outcome

Successfully diagnosed and replaced the faulty power supply, restoring the computer to normal operation.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Learned how to test a PSU using diagnostic tools.
- Practiced identifying faulty power components.
- Improved skills in power supply replacement and cable connections.
