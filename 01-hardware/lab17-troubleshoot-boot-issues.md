# Lab 17 – Troubleshoot Boot Issues

## Scenario

As a computer repair technician, I was assigned to troubleshoot a computer that failed to boot after a power supply replacement. The system required checking power connections and SATA connections to restore normal operation.

---

## Objectives

- Diagnose boot problems after a PSU replacement.
- Verify power connections.
- Correct SATA cable connections.
- Restore system startup.

---

## Technologies

- ATX Power Supply
- 8-Pin CPU Power Connector
- SATA Power Connector
- SATA Data Cable
- Windows Operating System

---

## Skills Demonstrated

- Boot Troubleshooting
- Power Supply Installation Verification
- Storage Connection Troubleshooting
- Hardware Diagnostics

---

## Procedure

1. Attempted to power on the computer and confirmed the startup failure.
2. Verified the AC power connection and turned on the power supply switch.
3. Checked internal power connections and found:
   - Missing 8-pin CPU power connection
   - Missing SATA power connection to the primary hard drive
4. Connected the CPU power cable to the motherboard.
5. Connected SATA power to the operating system drive.
6. Restarted the computer and identified a missing boot device issue.
7. Checked SATA data connections and found the primary drive cable disconnected.
8. Connected the SATA data cable from the hard drive to the motherboard.
9. Powered on the system and verified Windows successfully loaded.

---

## Outcome

Successfully diagnosed and repaired multiple boot issues caused by incorrect power and SATA connections after a power supply replacement.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Learned how to troubleshoot startup failures after hardware replacement.
- Practiced verifying PSU and storage connections.
- Improved skills diagnosing boot device problems.
