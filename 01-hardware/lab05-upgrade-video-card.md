# Lab 5 – Upgrade a Video Card

## Scenario

As a computer repair technician, I was assigned to upgrade a customer's computer by replacing the existing video card with a more advanced graphics adapter.

---

## Objectives

- Remove an existing video card.
- Install a new PCIe graphics card.
- Connect the correct display cable.

---

## Technologies

- PCI Express Video Card
- HDMI Connection
- DVI Connection
- 6-Pin PCIe Power Connector

---

## Skills Demonstrated

- Graphics Card Replacement
- Hardware Installation
- Display Cable Configuration

---

## Procedure

1. Disconnected the existing monitor cable from the computer.
2. Removed the existing video card from the system.
3. Selected a new PCIe video card with HDMI support.
4. Installed the new graphics card into an available PCI Express slot.
5. Connected the 6-pin PCIe power connector to the video card.
6. Connected the monitor to the new video card using an HDMI cable.
7. Verified the display connection.

---

## Outcome

Successfully upgraded the computer with a new video card and configured HDMI video output.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Learned how to safely replace a graphics card.
- Practiced identifying video card features and power requirements.
- Improved skills connecting modern display interfaces.
