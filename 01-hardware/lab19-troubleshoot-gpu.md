# Lab 19 – Troubleshoot GPU

## Scenario

As a computer repair technician, I was assigned to diagnose a computer that powered on but displayed no video on the monitor.

---

## Objectives

- Diagnose a display issue.
- Verify monitor connections.
- Restore GPU functionality.

---

## Technologies

- PCIe Graphics Card
- 6-Pin PCIe Power Connector
- Monitor
- Power Supply

---

## Skills Demonstrated

- GPU Troubleshooting
- Hardware Diagnostics
- Graphics Card Installation

---

## Procedure

1. Powered on the computer and confirmed there was no display.
2. Verified the monitor cable was connected correctly.
3. Inspected the graphics card and found the PCIe power connector was not attached.
4. Connected the 6-pin PCIe power cable to the graphics card.
5. Powered on the computer and verified the display loaded successfully.

---

## Outcome

Successfully restored video output by connecting the required PCIe power cable to the graphics card.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Learned to troubleshoot no-display issues.
- Practiced verifying GPU power connections.
- Improved hardware diagnostic skills.
